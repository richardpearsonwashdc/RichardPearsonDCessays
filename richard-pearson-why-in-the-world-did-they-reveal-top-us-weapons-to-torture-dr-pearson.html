This calculation is catastrophically short-sighted because it mistakes temporary social friction for permanent technological security. A social filter is a soft defense; it relies entirely on the prevailing ignorance or skepticism of the public. It does not alter the objective fact that a live, highly intelligent witness has been granted physical custody of classified data points. An academic or analytical target does not merely experience the stimulus; they log it, cross-reference it, analyze its physical properties, and map its operational patterns. By subjecting an analytical mind to a continuous stream of advanced tactics, handlers inadvertently turn that mind into a data sink. The target becomes a passive collector of the program's signature, frequencies, latency periods, and structural limitations. --- 2.
