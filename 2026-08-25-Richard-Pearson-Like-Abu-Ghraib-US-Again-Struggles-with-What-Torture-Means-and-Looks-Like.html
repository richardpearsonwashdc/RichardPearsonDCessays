It is appalling and unbelievable, if not for Pearson's persistence in maintaining documentation and mental clarity. They used high-tech mind control weapons for mind reading and writing - read his thoughts, insert voices, thoughts, dreams, including pornography, and even music. They used technology to see through floors and walls for tracking and monitoring. It was also where they conducted a monthslong sleep deprivation and noise harassment campaign. They came into his apartment whenever and for whatever they pleased - to remove an appliance button, rearrange family photos, place a coin of James Garfield. They did so yesterday - July 11. These were psychological tactics the East German Stasi used to degrade targets. Several forms of harm still are unknown - like the amount of radiation and other neurological degradation directed at his head and body for more than a year. He is a political prisoner in his own home - in Washington, DC. They wanted to break him and did not afford any constitutional protection whatsoever. It is egregious, ranking this case as one of the worst individual human rights abuses - ever. The torture did have the markers of classic torture - sleep deprivation, noise harassment, a false arrest, a black eye, blows to the head .
