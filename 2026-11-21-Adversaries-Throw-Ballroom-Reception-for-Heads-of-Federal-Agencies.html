Adversaries Throw "Ballroom Reception" for Heads of Federal Agencies US Torture Victim Writes Part One of Satire Series The president of the host country rises to speak at the podium. The dais contains two dozen heads of state. "Welcome to tonight's 'ballroom' reception honoring the heads of forty federal agencies of our beloved foreign adversary. Forty! Tonight, we celebrate all of their successes, especially in the last two years. We could not be more grateful for their hard work and contribution to our collective national security and economic future." He gestures at the dais - "Collective." "I apologize that we could not host at the White House but, as you know, it is under construction." Laughter fills the ballroom. "It is slated to have 650 seats, though we have seen estimates in the press as high as 999. That's 666 inverted in case you didn't catch that. Somebody was really having some fun with this guy." Snickering in the room. "We personally invited our honorees. Each and every one of them marked it as spam. Can you believe it? We even said in the subject line, 'Be Our Guest. $1 Million Honorarium.' How did that not work? It's four times greater than their annual salary!" Boos from the audience. "Then, we decided that they needed more encouragement to accept our invitation. What did we do? We inserted voices in their heads. It is only fair, no? It appears that inserting voices, dreams, even music, is the preferred mode of discreet communication between countries. So what did we do? Before I tell you, I want to say that I had nothing to do with this. Absolutely nothing. I swear. I am very hands off with my intelligence department. I am reading from an intelligence report. I quote, 'For target 22, we inserted an AI-generated dream with his grandmother telling him to accept the invitation.' Did it work? Why yes!" Audience is clinking their glasses. "Target 22 retrieved the email, then spent three hours considering what to do. First, he took a picture with his personal cellphone, then spent the rest of the time drafting an email to the recipient from his personal email. Three hours. That's a long time, don't you think? Well, our team did some research to find out why. By research I mean, we just watched the guy struggle with writing. You'll be surprised how easy it is to hack someone's cellphone. This guy can't write! Can't spell either. He kept spelling his agency's name that contained the word 'tobacco' as 'toebacow'. T.O.E.B.A.C.OW." A roar of laughter. "He was very confident in his spelling, too. When the application flagged it as a misspelling, he added it to the dictionary! This guy's confidence is through the roof. I tell ya! I want whatever he is on. So, I asked my team to get his secret. As the head of state, I need all of the confidence that I can get. They came back to me the next day. You want to know what's behind his guy's self-confidence. I will just say. Powdery substance." Snort. Snort. "Then, after three hours, do you want to know what this guy wrote? 'I decline.' But there was something funny going on with his email. In his signature, he added an address. Was it his home address?" No!  His office address?  No!  We mapped it - a commercial dumpster behind the house of his mistress. We knew about the mistress. Not the dumpster. Hmmm. Why a dumpster? So brought up recordings from his phone and cameras that we have placed in his car and throughout his home. I should tell you that we were also reading this guy's mind. Long story. This guy wanted the money! For what?! For doing nothing! It was honorarium. We made ourselves clear. But we felt really bad for the guy. We pulled his credit report. This man is $4 million in debt, ok? That really pulled at our heartstrings. So, what did we do? A man yells from the front table, "A money drop!" Yup, a money drop. He took it. And where is he keeping $1 million in hard cash? His sock drawer! More laughter. "Alright, folks. I have had fun up here. But tonight is about our guests of honor. We are going to start with those agencies that really did a number on that African American guy in Washington, DC. Did you hear about it?" Tortured him! "They sure did! This guy has a PhD! Really smart dude. We watched the whole thing. It went on for so long that we ordered a popcorn machine! We didn't have to spend a penny. We could not have asked for a more generous gift from our adversary to benefit our collective interests." "Alright. First up. A man you've seen on American television - everyone's Favorite Uncle from the highly acclaimed reality show, I'm Broke - Mark Lawson. He's still broke, folks. But a million is a million, right, Mark?"
